Who we are

A small company with a builder's mindset.

Asvan Labs is an early-stage technology startup and part of JIRA Corp Private Limited. We are small on purpose: it keeps us close to the code, quick to act on feedback, and focused on doing a few things well.

We started with a problem we kept running into. Companies want AI tools to use their data, but the data sits in files, databases and warehouses that were never built to be shared safely. Teams end up writing one-off scripts and handing out credentials. Our first product, The Data-Bridge, is our answer to that, and it shapes how we will build everything after it.

The Data-Bridge by Asvan Labs Available

Governed, audited AI access to the data you already have.

Register a source once. People and AI agents then query it through SQL, REST and MCP by name, never by raw credentials.

  • GovernedRead-only SQL, per-key tools, column and row policies.
  • AuditedEvery query and AI tool call recorded; hash-chained in Enterprise.
  • Lean by designSmaller results for agents, so they spend fewer tokens.

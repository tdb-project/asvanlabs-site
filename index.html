Our product

The Data-Bridge (TDB)

Register a source once. Query it through SQL, REST and MCP.

TDB turns files, databases and warehouses into a read-only API that people and AI agents reach by a governed name, never by raw credentials.

  • GovernedRead-only SQL, per-key tools, column and row policies.
  • AuditedEvery query and AI tool call written to a signed log.
  • Lean by designSmaller results for agents, so they spend fewer tokens.
